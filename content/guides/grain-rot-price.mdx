## How much does GRAIN ROT cost?

The publisher's launch release lists a **US base price of $9.99**. Steam and the release announcement also recorded a 10% launch promotion, bringing the US price to **$8.99 through August 21, 2026**.

That promotion is time-limited. After its end date, a page should not call $8.99 the current price without reopening the store. Seasonal sales may later produce a different amount.
