## Windows requirements shown on Steam

The current page lists Windows 10 64-bit, 8 GB RAM, DirectX 11, and 10 GB available storage among its minimum requirements. Those values describe the Windows Steam build. They should not be expanded into an official macOS, Linux, or Steam Deck support claim without a separate developer listing.

The Steam page links to the official Discord, TikTok, Instagram, X, and Reddit presence. If a social invite changes, the Steam page is the best fallback for finding the developer-linked destination.
