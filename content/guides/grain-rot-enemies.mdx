## What are the enemies and monsters in GRAIN ROT?

Players commonly search for GRAIN ROT **enemies** or **monsters**. Official material calls the hostile creatures in the Deep Layers **Corrupted vessels**. The publisher explains them as Vessels that lost their Sparks, or did not lose them completely. They react to **sound, movement, and each other** in ways designed to be unpredictable.

That is the reliable detection boundary. The public official text does not define one shared vision cone, hearing radius, aggro duration, pack-coordination system, or flashlight rule for every enemy.

## What version 1.0 officially added

The July 14 launch preview confirms that the full release added:

- New enemies beyond the Demo.
- A Boss Encounter.
- Elite Encounters.
- Two additional biomes.

It does not name every added threat or publish a total roster size. A Demo bestiary therefore cannot be labeled “all full-release enemies.”

## Demo community bestiary

A June 23 Demo video shows eight visually distinct threats. The creator explicitly invents or prefers several names, so this table records **community labels**, not canon names.

| Community label | Demo video observation | Evidence limit |

| Plague Doctor / Soul Reaper | Separates a Spark from its Vessel and carries it away | Creator-selected name; full-release behavior not reverified |
| Eye / Cyclops | Pulling gaze and a ranged beam shown; walls interrupt the filmed attack | Name and exact counter are community observations |

| Crawler | Low-profile chase and leap | Claims of permanent tracking are not official |

## Universal survival rules that stay inside the evidence

1. **Listen and watch before committing.** Sound and movement are officially part of enemy reactions.
2. **Keep a retreat path.** Procedural rooms and unpredictable reactions make rigid scripts fragile.
3. **Use the environment.** Official media shows furniture, traps, dropkicks, coordinated attacks, and retreat.
4. **Protect Spark recovery.** A fight that isolates the only recoverable teammate can become a crew wipe.
5. **Do not force every encounter.** Extraction is part of the core loop, not a failure to fight.

## What this guide will not invent

There is no official table for enemy health, damage, drop rates, spawn depth, aggro seconds, or full-release weaknesses. “Three-sense detection,” a fixed Grinder spawn trigger, and a reusable “noise budget” appear on fan pages but not in the reviewed developer material.

Similarly, the statement that Corrupted react to each other does not prove they always coordinate, flank, or share complete aggro information.
