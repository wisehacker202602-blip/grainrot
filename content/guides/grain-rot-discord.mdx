## Verified invite

Use [discord.gg/xxHrFJTt3J](https://discord.gg/xxHrFJTt3J). The Steam store links this invite from its official social section. During the August 11 review, it redirected to a Discord invite page titled **GRAIN ROT**.

Member and online counts change continuously, so this guide does not freeze them into a permanent fact.

## How to verify the server

1. Open the invite from this page or the official Steam listing.
2. Confirm that the destination is titled **GRAIN ROT**.
3. If Discord says the invite expired, return to Steam rather than trying random invite directories.
4. Review the current server rules and role setup after joining.

The Reddit FAQ search summary has pointed new users toward a roles channel, but internal channel names and moderation workflows can change. This guide does not invent a permanent channel map.

- Official announcements and patch discussion.
- Finding other players.
- Asking for current-version help.
- Reporting a reproducible game issue through the route the team requests.
- Tracking future trial, platform, or event news.

## Official server versus community servers

Steam Community discussions can contain regional, language-specific, or creator-run servers. Those may be useful, but they should not be labeled official unless the game or publisher links them.

For refunds, transactions, or Steam account problems, use Steam Support. A community server does not promise a customer-support response time, and this fan site cannot access game or Discord accounts.
