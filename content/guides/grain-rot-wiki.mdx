## Is there an official GRAIN ROT wiki?

No developer-endorsed wiki was found during the August 11 review. The Steam store's official social links include Discord, TikTok, Instagram, X, and Reddit, but no wiki.

Several unrelated fan domains rank for “GRAIN ROT wiki.” A logo, official screenshot, strong Google position, or official-sounding domain does not establish developer ownership.

## Start with primary sources

Use this source order when a detail matters:

1. **Steam store and Steam News** for the official game description, features, and patches.
2. **Publisher or developer releases** for launch facts and roadmap announcements.
3. **Current full-release footage or screenshots** for reproducible UI and behavior.
4. **Official Discord and community discussions** for current reports that remain labeled as reports.
5. **Fan wikis** for discovering questions and organizing links, not as automatic proof.

## Independent sites currently in search

The reviewed results included `grainrotwiki.wiki`, `grainrot-wiki.wiki`, `grainrotwiki.org`, `grain-rot.wiki`, and `grainrot.online`. They vary in sourcing and caution.

Some pages clearly mark unknown data as `TBA` or explain that a name is community-created. Others publish detailed health, damage, spawn, weakness, control, or upgrade claims without an original source. A cross-check label can still point to another fan page, so follow the citation chain to its origin.

## High-risk topics to recheck

- Enemy names, health, damage, and weaknesses.
- Default controls after full release patches.
- Exact room levels, upgrade costs, and unlock tables.
- Drop rates and spawn triggers.
- “Best” builds and tier lists with no version.
- Demo behaviors presented as version 1.0 facts.

One reviewed fan page, for example, listed the flashlight key as F even though official and gameplay-video evidence supports R. A single conflict does not invalidate every page, but it shows why exact operations need a primary or reproducible source.
