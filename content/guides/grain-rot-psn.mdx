## Is there a GRAIN ROT PSN page?

No verified PSN listing was available during the August 11, 2026 review. Searching the PlayStation Store for “grain rot” can return partial-name matches, but none matched the full game title and confirmed developer information.

The official launch material names only **Windows PC through Steam**. It does not announce PS4, PS5, or PlayStation Network distribution.

## How to avoid the wrong store result

Names containing “Grain” or “Rot” are not enough. A real product page should match all of the following:

- Full title: **GRAIN ROT**.
- Developer: **Beck & Branch Games**.
- Publisher: **Neem Interactive**.
- A platform announcement linked from an official game or publisher channel.

Without that match, do not assume that a concept page, fan page, or similarly named game is the PlayStation edition.

## What is still unknown

There is no official PSN product identifier, regional price, PS Plus requirement, cross-play plan, trophy list, or release window. The existence of online co-op on Steam does not prove that a future console build would use the same networking or save system.

<FieldNote type="info" title="Current does not mean permanent">

“No PSN listing” describes the store on the review date. It should not be rewritten as a developer promise that PlayStation support will never happen.

</FieldNote>

## Where to watch for a real listing

Follow Steam News, the official GRAIN ROT Discord, and the publisher's announcements. If a PlayStation edition is revealed, use its first-party PlayStation Store page as the primary source rather than a search snippet.
