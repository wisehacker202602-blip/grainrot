## Current Xbox status

The official launch information lists **Windows PC via Steam** and does not list an Xbox console edition. Searches of Microsoft Store and Xbox.com did not return a matching official GRAIN ROT product during the August 11 review.

No Game Pass announcement was found either. A fan site's platform forecast is not a substitute for a Microsoft Store listing or a developer statement.

## Controller support is not an Xbox release

Steam marks GRAIN ROT with full controller support. That means a compatible controller can be used with the PC version; it does not mean the game runs on Xbox hardware.

This distinction matters because search snippets sometimes collapse “Xbox controller supported” into “Xbox supported.” A native console version would need its own store listing, platform requirements, certification, and release announcement.

## What has not been announced

- Xbox Series X|S or Xbox One release date.
- Game Pass or PC Game Pass availability.
- Console price.
- Cross-play with Steam.
- Cross-save or achievement transfer.
- Console performance modes.

<FieldNote type="warning" title="No date means no date">

Do not convert the PC launch date into a predicted Xbox date, and do not present an uncited year or quarter as a roadmap.

</FieldNote>

## How to track a future Xbox version

Check the exact-title results on Xbox.com and Microsoft Store, then confirm any new listing through Steam News or the official Discord. A valid page should identify Beck & Branch Games or Neem Interactive and should not rely only on copied PC screenshots.
