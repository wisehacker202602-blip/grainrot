## Is the full game free?

GRAIN ROT is **not free-to-play**. The official Windows PC release is a paid product on Steam. Its published US base price is $9.99, with a temporary launch discount documented separately in the [price guide](/platforms/price/).

The keyword “freetp” appears to be shorthand for “free to play.” It should not be interpreted as a special edition or platform.

## Why are there free-access results?

Three different things can appear in old results:

1. **A 24-hour Discord event.** Verified community members received temporary full-game access on August 1, before the public launch. The event ended.
2. **The pre-release Demo.** A separate Steam Demo was available around Steam Next Fest. Its public listing is no longer active.
3. **Unauthorized download pages.** These are not official distribution and should not be used.

<FieldNote type="warning" title="Avoid “free download” mirrors">

Only install the game through Steam or a destination linked by the developer or publisher. A page offering a cracked build, DLL pack, or unofficial installer is not evidence that the game is free-to-play.

</FieldNote>

## Does the Demo make it free-to-play?

No. A Demo is a limited promotional build, while free-to-play describes the business model of the released product. The current Steam store does not show a Demo button, and the retired Demo entry should not be promoted as a working download.

## Could it become free later?

Sales, temporary weekends, bundles, and future promotions are possible, but no reviewed official source announces a permanent free-to-play conversion. Check the live Steam listing rather than relying on an old event headline.
