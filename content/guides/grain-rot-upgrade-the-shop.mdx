## How Shop upgrades work

The Shop belongs to the persistent **Outpost room system**. Official Steam media shows furniture placement contributing to a `Room Level Up`, and the store describes returning loot as the way to unlock interactive furniture, stat upgrades, rescued survivors, and further progression.

Demo-era player observations add the practical sequence: repair the Shop area, clear the room, then build and place furniture or decoration to raise the room score. When the threshold is reached, the room levels up and the Shop can offer a higher equipment tier.

## Upgrade steps

1. **Bring resources home.** A failed run cannot fund the room.
2. **Locate and repair the Shop room.** Complete any visible restoration work.
3. **Clear usable space.** Remove or address debris that blocks furnishing.
4. **Build furniture with Scrap.** Choose pieces that the current build allows in that room.
5. **Place the items.** Watch the room rating and `Room Level Up` feedback.
6. **Recheck the Shop inventory.** Confirm what changed in your full-release version.

<FieldNote type="info" title="Why guides disagree">

The broad room-level mechanic is officially shown, but most exact Shop costs and unlock examples come from the June Demo. Version 1.0 may use different thresholds, item values, or inventory tiers.

</FieldNote>

## Is there an Upgrade Shop button?

No reviewed official source confirms a standalone `Upgrade Shop` menu action. The upgrade comes from improving the room itself. This is why a player can miss the solution while looking only inside a vendor interface.

## What does the first level unlock?

A Demo player reported more reliable defensive equipment, including examples such as a baseball bat and pistol. Treat those as community-observed Demo examples, not a guaranteed version 1.0 unlock table.

The official sources do not publish the maximum Shop level, exact Scrap cost per tier, required score, score per furniture item, or complete stock progression. A trustworthy future table should include screenshots from the current full release for every threshold.

## Which room should you prioritize?

There is no official universal ranking. A crew lacking basic equipment may value the Shop highly, while another run may benefit more from survivability, capacity, or a different persistent room. Use what the current crew repeatedly fails to do as the priority signal.
